I started off this year writing a lot, and wanted to keep up the pace. I did
good at first! And then... I didn't manage to.

In some ways, this is because I have just been super busy! I've never had less
free time, and I'm okay with that. There's a tremendous amount of good things
going on in my life.

But it's also put this whole Claude Code series in an awkward spot, because
things have been changing so rapidly, that it's been hard to keep up. And I also
need to figure out what it is I actually want to say, and my practice has
changed tremendously since January!

I'm thinking about this a lot today in part because one of the things that happened
since we last talked about this stuff was the release of Claude Fable. And now,
a new Fable has just been put out into the world, and OpenAI's Astra is coming very
soon. New models don't always mean that practice inherently changes, but like, it's
just feeling like this is getting further and further behind, and so I think it's
probably good for me to just officially close this series. I still really like what
I wrote, and think it's still valuable today for getting started, but I just don't
think I can commit to keeping up with documenting everything I do at the pace that
stuff is happening at. So being honest with myself about that is very useful.

To those that found this series helpful, I'm glad, and I'm sorry I'm not writing
more about it. I'll still write things about this topic in general, but the core
idea of a "how to get started doing this stuff" series just isn't in the cards
for me right now.

AI is here to stay, and it is dramatically changing how people work. I believe
that more strongly than ever. So stopping isn't about me somehow getting bearish
on this stuff. It's just that I'm too darn busy for this specific project.

This is also why the "notes" section on my website still has zero entries. I was
hoping that something to put stuff shorter form than a blog post would make it
easier to write, but in the end, I can't even find much time for that.

Okay well I'm having trouble wrapping this up so, I'll just leave it here for
now.
